# Historical sources and fact status

Fact status:
- **Confirmed**: two or more independent, reliable sources agree.
- **Range**: sources differ; the film uses the most-cited figure or states the range on screen.
- **Approx.**: positions or tracks drawn schematically and labelled as approximate on screen.

> Working note: in this production environment, web search works but direct page fetches from nps.gov, history.navy.mil, ibiblio.org, archives.gov and loc.gov are blocked. Facts were checked against the search excerpts of the sources below. They were not read from full primary texts, which should be re-checked once those hosts are reachable.

## Geodata and attribution

- Elevation and bathymetry: AWS Terrain Tiles ("terrarium"), from SRTM, GMTED, ETOPO1, USGS NED and others.
- Hawaii land polygons: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via simonepri/geo-maps.
- Pacific-scale coastlines: Natural Earth 10m (public domain).
- Pearl Harbor detailed shoreline: derived from the z15 DEM by `tools/geo/build_harbor_shoreline.py`. It is the **modern** shoreline; east of 157.925°W the harbor reverts to the coarse OSM polygon.
- Battleship Row positions (`src/data/berths.json`): placed along the derived Ford Island shore in the documented berthing order. They are **schematic**.
